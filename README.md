# Canadian Leopards for Gunner, HEAT, PC!

![A column of C1s ready to march](https://i.imgur.com/UE1ipHA.jpeg)  
  
This mod firstly overhauls the German Leopard 1A3 series of main battle tanks to represent Leopard C1s at CFB Lahr under the 4th Canadian Mechanized Brigade Group. This includes replacing the gunner's primary sight with an approximation of the SABCA Cobelda fire control system, adding a laser rangefinder and automatic lead calculation; enforcing the use of the PZB-200 night sight; replacing the MG3 machine-guns with the C6 (FN MAG, known to the Americans as the M240); alternating the suite of ammunition; removing and replacing decals, including troop callsigns on the turrets; and last but not least, replacing German crew voices with English ones. Additionally, the A1A1 and 1A4 Leopards with their cast turrets are used as a basis for representing the post-Cold War Leopard C2, complete with EMES-18 gunner's sight with thermal channel and improved stabilizers. 

Ammunition options are: "historical" per Canadian armour in the 80s (APDS or APFSDS and HESH only, challenging to use!), "American" (M774 APFSDS and M456A2 HEAT only), or "German" for the default German ammunitions (Sabot, HEAT and HESH). When using the Canadian loadout, DM23 is redesignated C76, DM13 becomes C35, and DM512 is L35.  

Cosmetic options allow the player to keep the default German base coat of "Gelboliv" (yellow-olive), which despite its name appears rather gray; or apply a NATO CARC Green colour on the older 1A1's. The 1A3 comes in olive drab. Additionally the chances that some vehicles appear in NATO "three-colour" camouflage can be disabled. Maple leaves and callsigns may be outlined in white or kept simply black. German-style tactical symbols may be worn, as they appear in some period photos on the bottom left corner of the upper front plate and rear tool box, taking the form of a NATO map symbol for an armoured battalion marked 'X 4' (for the 4th Brgiade) on the right-hand side. Additionally a Military Load Classification number of 46 is sometimes worn on the hull front, available in white outline or plain black. These two additions may be disabled in the config file.  

Version 1.4 adds the option to convert West German M113 APCs and infantry into Canadian assets, toggleable in the config. Toggling "additional decals" with remove the MLC number and NATO map symbols from the APC hull. 1.5 contributes a new model for the C1A1 rifle (FN FAL), and field dressings, which were often tapped onto the webbing at the left shoulder.

The Cobelda sight has two magnification levels and when you apply lead, the camera will seem to jump ahead to where the gun is aiming, while the graticule lags behind to remain on the moving target. When you fire then, the tracer will seem to precede the target and curve into it as you pan across. The lead will not update in real time however, so if the target changes direction or speed, you will need to dump lead, track and laze again to re-aquire the firing solution.   
The EMES-18 sight is nearly identical in appearance and function as the EMES-15 seen on the Leopard 2 family. It has a very narrow field of view with a single magnification in the day channel, while the thermal channel has a WFOV for scanning and NFOV for fine aiming. Dynamic lead allows you to maintain a track on a moving target of changing speed or direction and fire confidently. However be aware, if the distance to the target is changing quickly, you may will need to dump and relaze. Like the Leo 1a4 or Leo2, the C2's EMES-18 requires you to press and hold the laze button (E by default) or the dedicated lead button (capslock by default) in addition to the palm switches (right mouse by default). Readiness to fire, range, and ammunition index is displayed along the top of the sight instead of at the bottom.

***Installation***  
This mod requires MelonLoader and is intended to be used with Version 0.6.1!
This mod does NOT require the Vehicle Preloader or Texture Loader.

To install from the ZIP file, extract the "CanadianLeopards.dll" file and the "CanadianLeopards" folder into the /mods/ folder. If done correctly, there should only be PNGs and Unity Engine asset bundles (appearing as files without extensions) in the /mods/CanadianLeopards/ directory, while the DLL file is separate.

***Known Issues***  
- American voice acting doesn't accurately reflect Canadian armoured crew communication.
- The stereoscopic rangefinder will still appear in the AAR.
- The lead calculation on the C1s depicted does not perfectly match descriptions of the SABCA system.
- There may be compatibility problems with other mods that modify Leopards.
